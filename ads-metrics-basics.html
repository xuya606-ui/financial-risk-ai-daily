一、媒体与基础漏斗

CPM

Spend ÷ Impressions × 1000

千次展示成本。

CTR

Clicks ÷ Impressions

反映吸引力，不代表借款人质量。

CPC

Spend ÷ Clicks

CPI

Spend ÷ Installs

安装成本；需继续接 Apply/Disburse。

CPL

Spend ÷ Leads

线索成本，不等于首贷 CAC。

CVR

下一步完成数 ÷ 上一步人数

必须注明区间。

CPA

Spend ÷ Target Actions

必须注明事件：Install/Apply/Disburse。

Register Rate

Registered Users ÷ Valid Visits/First Launches

Apply Rate

Valid Applications ÷ Registered/Eligible Users

Approve Rate

Approved Applications ÷ Valid Applications

Disburse Rate

Disbursed ÷ Approved

First-loan CAC

Acquisition Ad Spend ÷ First Disbursed Borrowers

现金贷拉新核心经营成本。

二、页面与 Lead Quality

Cost per LPV

Spend ÷ Landing Page Views

Landing Page Load Rate

LPV ÷ Ad Clicks

Click→LPV Loss Rate

1 − LPV ÷ Ad Clicks

Valid Lead Rate

Valid Leads ÷ Raw Submitted Leads

Lead→KYC Rate

KYC Users ÷ Matched Leads

Lead→Disburse Rate

First Disbursed Borrowers ÷ Matched Leads

Lead-source First-loan CAC

Lead-source Spend ÷ First Disbursed Borrowers

Form Duplicate Rate

Duplicate Leads ÷ Raw Submitted Leads

三、出价与预算

tCPA

平台目标事件的平均目标成本

若优化 Apply，tCPA ≠ First-loan CAC。

tROAS

Target Conversion Value ÷ Spend

Target Gap Ratio

(Platform Target CPA − Mature Actual CPA) ÷ Mature Actual CPA

Business-derived Apply tCPA

Allowable First-loan CAC × Mature Apply→Disburse Rate

Extra Budget Utilization

实际 Extra Spend ÷ 配置的 Extra Budget Capacity

Incremental First-loan CAC

Expansion Spend ÷ Expansion-attributed First Disbursed Borrowers

Expansion FPD Delta

Expansion Mature FPD − Comparable Base Mature FPD

Expansion LTV/CAC

Expansion Risk-adjusted LTV ÷ Expansion Incremental CAC
TikTok 官方：Safe Budget Increase ｜ Smart+ Bid Protection

四、Measurement / Attribution

UTM Source Coverage

有效 utm_source Sessions ÷ Eligible Paid Sessions

UTM ID Spend Coverage

可由 utm_id 匹配 Spend ÷ Eligible Spend

Platform→GA4 Gap

(Platform Conversions − Comparable GA4 Events) ÷ Platform Conversions

GA4→Backend Apply Gap

(GA4 Matched Apply − Backend Valid Apply) ÷ GA4 Matched Apply

Event Coverage Rate

平台收到的去重有效事件 ÷ Backend Eligible Events

Attribution Window Consistency

已记录且完成可比映射的 Campaign/Event ÷ Eligible Campaign/Event

Apple Ads↔MMP Install Gap

(Apple Ads Reported Downloads − MMP Comparable Installs) ÷ Apple Ads Reported Downloads

只用于诊断差异；Apple Ads 以 App Store verified download、MMP 常以 First Open，定义与归因窗口可能不同。

P50/P90 Conversion Lag

50% / 90% 转化达到成熟所需时间。

Apple 官方：MMP measurement differences ｜ AdServices / AdAttributionKit

五、实验与增量

Experiment First-loan Lift

Trial First Loans ÷ Comparable Control First Loans − 1

Experiment Incremental CAC

(Trial Spend − Control Spend) ÷ (Trial First Loans − Control First Loans)

Risk-adjusted Incremental ROAS

Incremental Risk-adjusted Lifetime Value ÷ Incremental Ad Spend

Experiment Maturity Coverage

达到规定风险成熟窗口的 Cohorts ÷ Eligible Cohorts

Forecast Error

(Actual − Forecast) ÷ Forecast

Guardrail Compliance Rate

持续满足 URL/Disclosure/Policy Guardrail 的 Delivery ÷ Eligible Delivery
Google 官方：AI Max testing & planning tools ｜ Measurement Suite

Search Impression Share

Captured Eligible Search Impressions ÷ Eligible Search Impressions

Top 1 Impression Share

Captured Eligible Top-1 Impressions ÷ Eligible Top-1 Impressions

Search→Disburse Rate

Search-attributed First Disbursed ÷ Matched Search Clicks/Visits

Search First-loan CAC

Search Spend ÷ Search-attributed First Disbursed Borrowers

Search FPD Delta

Search Mature FPD − Comparable Non-search Mature FPD
TikTok 官方：Impression Share ｜ TopSearch

七、Automation Control

Automation Surface Coverage

Enabled Automated Surfaces ÷ Governed Automation Surfaces

建议六个 Surface：Audience、Budget、Placement、Bid/Optimization、Creative、Destination。不是越高越好，用于描述自动化程度。

Manual Override Rate

Manual / Controlled Surfaces ÷ Governed Automation Surfaces

衡量人工强约束比例；与 Automation Surface Coverage 成对看。

Automation-version First-loan CAC

Spend under automation_version ÷ First Disbursed Borrowers

用配置版本解释 CAC，而不是只写 Smart+ / Manual。

Auto Expansion FPD Delta

Higher-automation Cohort Mature FPD − Comparable Control Mature FPD

判断自动扩量是否带来边际风险恶化。

Destination Guardrail Pass Rate

通过 Product/Disclosure/URL QA 的 Automated Destinations ÷ Automated Destination Candidates

金融广告建议以 100% 为内部硬门槛。

Creative Combination Concentration

Top N Creative Combinations Spend ÷ Total Automated Creative Spend

识别系统是否过度集中到少数组合；N 应固定。

TikTok 官方：Upgraded Smart+ ｜ Smart+ Targeting

八、App Store / Custom Product Page

App Store Conversion Rate

Total Downloads ÷ Unique Impressions

Apple App Store Connect Analytics 官方口径。与内部“产品页浏览→下载率”不同。

Product Page View→Download Rate（内部）

Downloads ÷ Unique Product Page Views

用于诊断进入商店页后的承接效率；不要把它命名成 Apple 官方 Conversion Rate。

Creative→CPP Message Match Coverage

已映射并通过 QA 的 Creative/Angle ÷ Eligible Creative/Angle

衡量高量创意是否都有与承诺一致的 CPP/Store Destination；金融场景建议内部目标 100%。

CPP First-loan CAC

Spend routed to CPP ÷ First Disbursed Borrowers from matched CPP cohort

判断某个 Custom Product Page 是否真正带来更便宜的健康首贷。

Campaign Link Coverage

使用标准 Campaign Link/Token 的 Eligible iOS Campaigns ÷ Eligible iOS Campaigns

用于保证外部营销活动能够在 App Store Connect Analytics 中按 Campaign 追踪。

CPP FPD Delta

CPP Cohort Mature FPD − Comparable Default Page Cohort Mature FPD

判断商店页优化是否吸引了风险更差的借款人。

First-Time Download Share

First-Time Downloads ÷ Total Downloads

区分真正新下载与 Redownload，避免把再下载量误当新增获客。

Apple 官方：Acquisition Analytics ｜ Campaign Links ｜ Custom Product Pages

九、2026-09-20 新增：Journey-aware Bidding / Goal Architecture

Journey Signal Coverage

可完整匹配到规定 Event Journey 的 Eligible Paid Users ÷ Eligible Paid Users

衡量从广告触点到 Register/KYC/Apply/Approve/Disburse 的链路是否完整。今天内部治理建议先以 ≥95% 为起点，而非平台官方 Benchmark。

Primary→Disburse Yield

First Disbursed Borrowers ÷ Primary Optimization Events

判断当前 Primary Event 与真实首贷之间的业务产出率；适合比较不同 Goal Architecture。

Biddable Goal Purity

通过 Marketing-safe 定义与字段 QA 的 Biddable Conversions ÷ All Biddable Conversions

用于防止 Goal 配置中混入定义漂移、重复或敏感数据字段；金融场景建议内部目标 100%。

Goal-version First-loan CAC

Spend under goal_version ÷ First Disbursed Borrowers under comparable cohort

每次 Primary/Secondary、Custom Goal、Optimization Event 调整都生成 goal_version，避免把配置变化误解成渠道质量变化。

Deep-event Lag P50/P90

广告/上游事件 → 指定 Deep Event 的 50% / 90% 成熟时间

用于判断更深事件是否足够及时参与优化与复盘；不同事件需分别计算。

Google 官方：Lead intent scoring / journey-aware bidding（2026-09-18） ｜ Journey-aware bidding beta ｜ Primary / Secondary

十、风险与价值

FPD

First Payment Default。必须标明 FPD1/3/7/30、件数/金额口径、成熟期。

Vintage

按放款 Cohort、账龄/MOB 比较风险；跨渠道必须同成熟度。

Risk-adjusted LTV

生命周期收入 − 预期信用损失 − 资金成本 − 服务/催收等变动成本

LTV/CAC

Risk-adjusted LTV ÷ First-loan CAC

Payback

累计风险调整贡献首次 ≥ CAC 时的天数

ROAS

Attributed / Incremental Value ÷ Ad Spend

必须说明 Value 口径。

ROI

(收益 − 总成本) ÷ 总成本

现金贷推荐判断顺序

  1. 先确认 Policy / Licensing / Disclosure。
  2. 确认 Measurement Health、Goal Architecture、Primary/Secondary 与数据边界。
  3. 看媒体效率，并验证 Ad→Store/LP→App 的承接链路。
  4. 必须下钻到 Apply→Approve→Disburse；扩量、自动化和 Goal 变更都用 version / experiment 做对照。
  5. 最终用同成熟窗口的 FPD/Vintage、Risk-adjusted LTV、Payback/ROI 决策。
