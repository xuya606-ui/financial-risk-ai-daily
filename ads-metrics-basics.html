一、媒体与漏斗基础

CPM

Spend ÷ Impressions × 1000

千次展示成本，只说明曝光采购成本。

CTR

Clicks ÷ Impressions

反映素材吸引力，不代表借款人质量。

CPC

Spend ÷ Clicks

CPI

Spend ÷ Installs

必须继续接 Backend Valid Install、Apply、Approve、Disburse。

CPL

Spend ÷ Leads

线索成本，不等于首贷获客成本。

CVR

下一步完成数 ÷ 上一步人数

必须标明区间，如 Click→LPV、Apply→Disburse。

CPA

Spend ÷ Target Actions

必须注明事件名：Install/Apply/Disburse CPA 不可混用。

Register Rate

Registered Users ÷ Valid Visits/First Launches

Application Rate

Valid Applications ÷ Registered/Eligible Users

Approve Rate

Approved Applications ÷ Valid Applications

Disburse Rate

Disbursed ÷ Approved（或团队固定另一口径）

First-loan CAC

Acquisition Ad Spend ÷ First Disbursed Borrowers

现金贷拉新核心经营指标。

二、Lead Quality / Higher Intent

Form Completion Rate

Submitted Leads ÷ Form Opens

衡量表单摩擦；Higher Intent 通常可能降低完成率，但不代表经营变差。

Valid Lead Rate

Valid Leads ÷ Raw Submitted Leads

Valid Lead 应由团队统一定义，例如手机号/邮箱可用、非重复、非机器人、字段完整。

Form Duplicate Rate

Duplicate Leads ÷ Raw Submitted Leads

用于识别重复提交、CRM 去重与低质量线索。

Lead→KYC Rate

KYC Started/Completed Users ÷ Matched Leads

判断线索是否真正进入借款产品流程。

Lead→Apply Rate

Backend Valid Applications ÷ Matched Leads

比 Form CPL 更接近现金贷真实转化。

Lead→Disburse Rate

First Disbursed Borrowers ÷ Matched Leads

连接营销线索与首贷结果；需明确 Lead 去重与归因规则。

Lead-source First-loan CAC

Lead-source Spend ÷ First Disbursed Borrowers from Matched Leads

用于比较 More Volume / Higher Intent / Website Lead / Instant Form 的真实获客成本。

Higher-Intent Uplift

Treatment Quality Metric ÷ Control Quality Metric − 1

建议分别计算 Valid Lead、Lead→Disburse、FPD/LTV Uplift；不能只看 CPL。

CRM Sync Success Rate

成功进入 CRM 且字段映射完整的 Leads ÷ Platform Submitted Leads

用于排查 Webhook/API/字段映射问题。

TikTok 官方：Instant Form ｜ Smart+ Lead Generation

三、Measurement Quality

UTM Source Coverage Rate

带有效 utm_source 的 Eligible TikTok Sessions ÷ Eligible TikTok Paid Sessions

UTM Source Accuracy Rate

utm_source 正确映射为 TikTok 的 Sessions ÷ 带 utm_source 的 TikTok Paid Sessions

UTM ID Spend Coverage

能通过 utm_id 匹配到 GA4 的 TikTok Campaign Spend ÷ Eligible TikTok Web Spend

Platform→GA4 Conversion Gap

(Platform Attributed Conversions − GA4 Comparable Key Events) ÷ Platform Attributed Conversions

Gap 大不等于 Fraud。

GA4→Backend Apply Gap

(GA4 Matched Apply − Backend Valid Apply) ÷ GA4 Matched Apply

Measurement Match Coverage

可在 Platform/GA4/MMP/Backend 至少三层完成匹配的 Eligible Spend ÷ Total Eligible Spend

Measurement Drift Rate

当前 Reporting Gap − 自身历史基线 Gap

Event Definition Consistency Rate

定义与触发逻辑已对齐的关键事件 ÷ 全部用于投放/经营的关键事件

Attribution Window Consistency Rate

有明确记录并完成可比映射的 Campaign/Event ÷ Eligible Campaign/Event

Reporting Timezone Consistency

按统一业务日完成转换的报表/数据源 ÷ Eligible Data Sources
TikTok 官方：TikTok Ads × Google Analytics Reporting Comparison

四、Landing Page / Web-to-App

Cost per LPV

Spend ÷ Landing Page Views

Landing Page Load Rate

LPV ÷ Ad Clicks

Click-to-LPV Loss Rate

1 − LPV ÷ Ad Clicks

LPV→Apply Rate

Backend Valid Apply ÷ Matched LPV

LPV→First-loan Rate

First Disbursed Borrowers ÷ Matched LPV

五、Creative / Language / Lead Quality

Qualified Lead Rate

Qualified Leads ÷ Leads

Creative First-loan CAC

Creative Spend ÷ Creative-attributed First Disbursed Borrowers

Asset Compliance Coverage Rate

具备有效审核+disclosure_version 的 Eligible Assets ÷ Selectable Assets

Language Match Rate

广告语言与 LP/App Locale 一致的可测会话 ÷ 可测 Localized Sessions

Localized Asset Compliance Pass Rate

通过 Language QA + Compliance Lock 的 Variants ÷ 已审核 Localized Variants

Localized First-loan CAC

Language/Locale Variant Spend ÷ 对应 First Disbursed Borrowers

Translation Incident Rate

重大金融语义翻译事故 Variants ÷ 已审核 Localized Variants

六、出价与预算基础

tCPA

平台目标事件的平均目标成本

若优化 Apply，tCPA 不等于首贷 CAC。

tROAS

目标 Conversion Value ÷ Spend

Target Gap Ratio

(Platform Target CPA − Mature Actual CPA) ÷ Mature Actual CPA

Business-derived Apply tCPA

Allowable First-loan CAC × Mature Apply→Disburse Rate

七、2026-09-15 新增：预算扩量与边际效率

Extra Budget Utilization Rate

实际使用的 Extra Spend ÷ 配置的 Extra Budget Capacity

判断额外预算是否真正被系统使用；低利用率可能说明系统没有足够稳定信号或目标成本约束过紧。

Incremental First-loan CAC

Expansion Spend ÷ Expansion-attributed First Disbursed Borrowers

扩量最关键的边际成本指标。Blended CAC 健康不代表 Incremental CAC 健康。

Scale Efficiency Ratio

Base Mature First-loan CAC ÷ Expansion Incremental First-loan CAC

越接近 1 表示扩量边际效率越接近 Base；显著低于 1 代表新增预算越来越贵。

Expansion FPD Delta

Expansion Cohort Mature FPD − Comparable Base Cohort Mature FPD

判断扩量是否以更差的还款质量换规模；必须保持相同 FPD 定义和成熟度。

Expansion Risk-adjusted LTV/CAC

Expansion Cohort Risk-adjusted LTV ÷ Expansion Incremental First-loan CAC

用于决定新增预算是否还有足够单位经济性。

Bid Protection Eligibility Rate

满足 Bid Protection 条件的 Eligible Days ÷ Safe Budget Increase Active Days

只能用于运营监控，不能把广告金资格当作业务质量指标。

Budget Change Maturity Coverage

已达到业务成熟窗口的 Budget Versions ÷ 已启用 Budget Versions

避免用未成熟 Cohort 对新增预算过早下结论。

TikTok 官方：Safe Budget Increase ｜ Smart+ Bid Protection

八、归因、iOS 与反作弊

Attribution Window Expansion Lift

Long-window Conversions ÷ Short-window Conversions − 1

只代表归因覆盖变化，不等于真实新增。

MMP-to-Backend Install Gap

(MMP Installs − Backend Verified First Launches) ÷ MMP Installs

P50/P90 Conversion Lag

50%/90% 转化成熟所需时间，用于决定复盘窗口。

Event Coverage Rate

平台收到的去重有效事件 ÷ Backend Eligible Events

Verified Postback Rate

Signature-verified Unique Postbacks ÷ Unique Received Postbacks

Fine CV Availability Rate

Valid Postbacks with Fine CV ÷ Valid Postbacks

Fraud Block Rate

Real-time Blocked Fraud ÷ Eligible Attempts

Post-attribution Fraud Rate

Later-confirmed Fraud ÷ Attributed Events

九、Publisher / Placement

Publisher ID Coverage Rate

带有效 Publisher ID 的 Network Spend ÷ Network Spend

Publisher First-loan CAC

Publisher Spend ÷ Publisher First Disbursed Borrowers

Publisher FPD7 Delta

Publisher Mature FPD7 − Comparable Baseline FPD7

十、风险与价值

FPD

First Payment Default。必须标明 FPD1/3/7/30、件数/金额口径和成熟期。

Vintage

按放款 Cohort、账龄/MOB 比较风险表现；跨渠道必须同成熟度。

Risk-adjusted LTV

生命周期收入 − 预期信用损失 − 资金成本 − 服务/催收等变动成本

LTV/CAC

Risk-adjusted LTV ÷ First-loan CAC

Payback

累计风险调整贡献首次 ≥ CAC 时对应天数

ROI

(收益 − 总成本) ÷ 总成本

十一、合规资产

Compliance Asset Coverage Rate

具备所需 Approved Compliance Assets 的 Active Campaigns ÷ Required Campaigns

FUE Disclosure Pass Rate

通过贷款披露检查的 FUE URL ÷ 已审计 FUE URL

Disclosure Version Coverage Rate

拥有有效 disclosure_version 的 Regulated Assets/URLs ÷ 全部 Regulated Assets/URLs

现金贷推荐阅读顺序

  1. 先看 Policy / Compliance。
  2. 再看 Measurement Health。
  3. 再看媒体效率。
  4. 下钻 Valid Lead/KYC/Apply/Approve/Disburse 和 First-loan CAC。
  5. 扩量时单独看 Incremental CAC、Expansion FPD/Vintage 与 LTV/CAC。
  6. 最终用成熟 Risk-adjusted LTV/Payback/ROI 决策预算。

所有指标进入经营看板时，应标注事件定义、分母、时间窗口、成熟度、归因来源、Campaign Type、Optimization Event、Attribution Window、Timezone、Fraud 处理、creative_id、form_id、publisher_id、lp_version、disclosure_version、budget_version、schema_version 和 metric_version。
