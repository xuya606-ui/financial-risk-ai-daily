核心链路

CPM → CTR/CPC → Click → LPV → CPI/CPL/CPA → Register → KYC → Apply → Approve → Disburse → First-loan CAC → FPD/Vintage → LTV/CAC → Payback → ROI

CPC

Spend ÷ Clicks

CPI

Spend ÷ Installs

CPA

Spend ÷ Target Actions

First-loan CAC

Spend ÷ First Disbursed Borrowers

ROAS

Attributed Value ÷ Ad Spend

FPD

首期逾期人数或金额 ÷ 到期首期人数或金额

Vintage

同一放款 Cohort 随账龄变化的风险表现

Automation Surface Coverage

Enabled Automated Surfaces ÷ Governed Automation Surfaces

衡量 Audience、Budget、Placement、Bid、Creative、Destination 六类自动化面中，已开放自动化的比例。

Automation-version CAC

Spend under automation_version ÷ First Disbursed Borrowers

Auto Expansion FPD Delta

Higher-automation Cohort FPD − Comparable Control FPD

Placement Mix

各 Placement Spend / Impressions / Disburse 占比

Destination Guardrail Pass Rate

合规 Destination Delivery ÷ Automated Destination Candidates

Journey Signal Coverage

能完整匹配规定事件旅程的付费用户 ÷ Eligible Paid Users

衡量 Register→KYC→Valid Apply→Approve→Disburse 等事件链的可匹配程度。

Dedup Coverage

成功通过去重校验的目标事件 ÷ 目标事件总量

Primary→Disburse Yield

First Disbursed Borrowers ÷ Primary Optimization Events

Deep-event Lag P50/P90

深层事件从发生到平台接收的中位数/90分位延迟

Lead Authenticity Rate

Technically Valid Leads ÷ Raw Submitted Leads

Version-aware CAC

同一 OS/App/SDK/产品版本 Cohort Spend ÷ First Disbursed Borrowers
